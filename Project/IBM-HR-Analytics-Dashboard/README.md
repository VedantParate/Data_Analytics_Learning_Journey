# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard analyzes employee attrition, workforce demographics, job roles, departments, overtime, age groups, and other employee-related factors to identify patterns associated with employee turnover.

The objective is to transform raw HR data into an interactive and business-focused dashboard that supports data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Employee attrition can have a significant impact on recruitment costs, workforce productivity, and organizational stability.

This project aims to analyze employee data and answer key HR business questions related to employee attrition.

---

## ❓ Business Questions

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which job roles experience higher employee turnover?
- Does age influence employee attrition?
- Is attrition different across genders?
- Does overtime increase employee attrition?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition?
- Does business travel have an impact on employee attrition?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Employees KPI
- Attrition Count KPI
- Attrition Rate KPI
- Average Monthly Income KPI
- Average Age KPI
- Department-level attrition analysis
- Job Role attrition analysis
- Gender-based attrition analysis
- Age Group analysis
- Overtime analysis
- Interactive Department slicer
- Interactive OverTime slicer
- Interactive Gender slicer
- Interactive Job Role slicer
- Key Insights section

---

## 💡 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

- Total employees analyzed: **1,470**
- Employees who left the organization: **237**
- Overall attrition rate: **16.12%**
- Sales recorded the highest department attrition rate at **20.63%**
- Employees working overtime had a **30.53%** attrition rate compared with **10.44%** for employees who did not work overtime.
